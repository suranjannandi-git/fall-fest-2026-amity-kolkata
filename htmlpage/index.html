About Us

🌐 About Qiskit Fall Fest

Qiskit Fall Fest is a global collection of quantum computing events hosted by IBM Quantum Network universities around the world. The event series invites students, researchers, and enthusiasts to explore the frontiers of quantum computing through hands-on workshops, talks, and challenges.

🏛️ Our Organization

[ORGANIZATION NAME / CLUB TBD] at Amity University Kolkata is dedicated to fostering a culture of quantum computing education and research. We bridge the gap between theoretical foundations and practical quantum applications.

🎯 Our Vision

To create a vibrant quantum computing community at Amity University Kolkata and empower the next generation of quantum engineers, researchers, and innovators.

🚀 Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality quantum computing education through events, workshops, and resources — connecting local talent with the global quantum ecosystem.
